About ME

I completed the International Baccalaureate (IB) program in high school, which built the foundation for how I approach problems — methodically, but always curious. That curiosity followed me to TCU, where I'm pursuing a degree in Digital Culture and Data Analytics with a business minor.

What I've loved most about this degree is how it's let me explore both sides of my brain. On one hand, I've learned to work with real data — using Python and Pandas to clean and analyze datasets, applying statistical models like OLS regression, and building interactive visualizations in Tableau to tell stories numbers alone can't. On the other, the "Digital Culture" side of my degree has let me dig into the creative and human side of data: how people interact with technology, how design shapes the way information is understood, and how businesses use both art and analytics to connect with people.

I'm currently building on that technical foundation by learning SQL, and I'm excited to keep growing as someone who can bridge the gap between raw data and the creative, strategic thinking that makes it useful.

Outside of school, I work part-time at Common Grounds, where I've built strong communication skills and learned to stay organized under pressure.
