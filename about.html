About Me

From Western Colorado to TCU: blending quantitative analytics, brand strategy, and web architecture.

Roots & Education

Growing up in Grand Junction, Colorado, taught me to appreciate clear structure and disciplined execution. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Digital Culture and Data Analytics with a General Business minor at Texas Christian University (expected May 2027).

My analytical work focuses on quantitative modeling, cost behavior analysis, and macro-environmental frameworks like PESTEL, which I pair with front-end web technologies to turn complex datasets into clear, accessible interfaces.

Professional Experience

During my brand internship at The Lewis Agency, I created digital content strategies and cross-platform advertising assets for high-impact campaigns, including a 50th-anniversary celebration. I managed targeted email outreach and evaluated audience engagement to ensure our digital narratives aligned with core client goals.

In addition to agency work, I manage high-volume customer transactions, monitor inventory turnover, and streamline production workflows as a barista at Common Grounds in Fort Worth.

Community & Leadership

Beyond academic and professional projects, I participate in weekly community programming, small-group leadership discussions, and peer networking initiatives with Paradox College, partnering with student leaders to support event logistics and outreach efforts.
